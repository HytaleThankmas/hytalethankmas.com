# Hytale Thankmas

- Brand identity lives in `brand/` (designer SVG exports + README). Colors: flat-gold `#ffc864` + pine-green `#1e3228`; logotype font is Salsa.

- Hytale Thankmas is a yearly charity fundraising event by the Hytale Community
- We are part of Jacksepticeye's Thankmas
- 2026 will be our 7th year
- previously we raised:
  - 2021: 16805$ for New Story
  - 2022: 7500$ for World Central Kitchen
  - 2023: 10962$ for World Central Kitchen
  - 2024: 15200$ for Tiltify Impact Fund
  - 2025: 17841$ for World Central Kitchen
  - 2026: TBD for Make a Wish Foundation
- this year the event will be on the 12th of december
- our event usually is made up of a 8+ hour livestream featuring members of the hytale community, some panels, some games, etc and a in-game part where players can participate in games. the in-game part used to be on minecraft so far, but now that hytale got released we are finally able to do it on hytale this year.
- we collect donations through tiltify, so we dont handle the money
